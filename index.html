COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I ask about a funeral package?

Visit the Packages page to compare the proposed options, then use the Enquiry form to tell us which package you would like to discuss.

Can I request a personalised arrangement?

Yes. The Personalised Package is intended for families who need to discuss specific requirements. Include details in the enquiry form.

Where is Koena Dignity based?

The proposed service is based in Bloemfontein, Free State. A verified street address will be added once confirmed.

How can I contact the business?

You can use the Contact page, call the displayed telephone number or email the listed address. Please note that these contact details should be confirmed before the site goes live.
